    How to play

    Pack the bag in four easy steps.

    1. 1Click/drag the item

      Hold the mouse button on an item in your list, or simply click it, to pick it up.

    2. 2Move it towards the bag

      Drag it over the bag and let go on empty cells. Drag it out again to take it back.

    3. 3Rotate based on the perspective

      Press R or scroll the mouse wheel to turn the item until it fits the space.

    4. 4Do the others before the time runs out

      Pack every MUST item, then press Finish packing before the timer hits zero.

    5. !Note

      Make sure to avoid putting unnecessary things in the bag!

      Junk items like trash, receipts and wrappers take up space and reduce your points.

    About

    Fit-N-Pack is a drag-and-drop game developed by BTLED - ICT - 3A students. The game focuses on fitting everyday essentials/items into a single bag.

    Game Mechanics:

    In order to fit the everyday essentials, the players must carefully place items (e.g smartphone, lipstick, charger, etc.) into the bag cells before the time runs out. The items labeled with "must" are required to be placed inside. Once finished, click the "Finish Packing" button to finalize your bag. NOTE: Be careful upon placing items because there are unwanted junk/items around that can reduce your points.
