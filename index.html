    About

    Fit-N-Pack is a drag-and-drop game developed by BTLED - ICT - 3A students. The game focuses on fitting everyday essentials/items into a single bag.

    Game Mechanics:

    In order to fit the everyday essentials, the players must carefully place items (e.g smartphone, lipstick, charger, etc.) into the bag cells before the time runs out. The items labeled with "must" are required to be placed inside. Once finished, click the "Finish Packing" button to finalize your bag. NOTE: Be careful upon placing items because there are unwanted junk/items around that can reduce your points.
